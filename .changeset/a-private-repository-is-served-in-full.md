A private repository is served in full on every ref, whatever its licenses say (ADR-0062): its restrictive skills are loaded whole, their files read and the skills extension lists them, as for a repository the operator vouches for, since the people it is served to have it at the host already. A public repository nobody vouched for still describes a restrictive skill and keeps its files at the source.
