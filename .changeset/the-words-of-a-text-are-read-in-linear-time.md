The words of a text are read in time that grows with its length, whatever the text is: a long line of underscores, dots or hyphens, or of letters joined by plus signs, was read again from each of its characters and took minutes for a file of a megabyte, and a long run of versions joined by hyphens was read by a call for each and overflowed the stack, which failed the index of the whole pack. The address pattern takes at most the 64 characters a mailbox has before its sign and the URL pattern a scheme of at most 40, and a run is read in a loop.
