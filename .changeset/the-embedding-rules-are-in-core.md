The rules of what is embedded for the search by meaning are in `core`, for a deployment's worker and the `skillcdn` command to share (ADR-0064): `embeddable` names the entries that have sections to embed, `meaningSearchOf` reads the manifests of an index into whether each path's documents are embedded, and `embeddingBatches` packs texts into the calls an embedder takes at once.
