# Reading this pack

The pack is SkillCDN's documentation, under `docs/`; `docs/README.md` says what each file is for. The guides say how a pack is written and used, the specifications say what SkillCDN reads and serves today, and the decision records say why. A specification is current; a decision record may since have been superseded, so quote the specification for how something behaves now.
