# Commit messages

Write the summary line first: `type(scope): summary`, imperative mood, 72 characters or fewer.

1. Pick the type from the change itself: `feat`, `fix`, `docs`, `refactor`, `test`, `build`, `ci` or `chore`.
2. Name the scope after the part of the codebase that changed.
3. Use the body to say why the change was made, not what the diff already shows.

Before you finish, go through [the checklist](references/checklist.md).
