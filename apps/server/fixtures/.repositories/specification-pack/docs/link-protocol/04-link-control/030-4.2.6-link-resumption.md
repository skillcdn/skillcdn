### 4.2.6 Link resumption

A link that was lost is taken up again where it stood, so that nothing measured in between is lost with it. Resumption is the buoy's to begin and the shore station's to accept.

#### 4.2.6.1 Initiation

A buoy that detects the loss of its carrier, as [4.2.7](040-4.2.7-to-4.2.8-carrier-loss.md#4.2.7) defines it, enters HELD and sends a LinkResumeRequest in its next window. The request carries the number of the last message the buoy knows to have been heard.

#### 4.2.6.2 Guard times

The buoy waits g21 after a request before it sends the next, and gives up after g22 in all. Both are fields of [ResumeGuardTimes](../06-messages/010-6.1-message-definitions.md#6.1.8), which the shore station sets at set-up. A shore station under load answers with a LinkResumeReject that names a longer g21.

#### 4.2.6.3 Failure of resumption

When g22 runs out the buoy returns to LISTEN and keeps its measurements. The shore station marks the buoy lost, and what it does next is said in [4.2.8](040-4.2.7-to-4.2.8-carrier-loss.md#4.2.8).
