### 4.2.7 Detection of carrier loss

A shore station counts the windows in which it hears neither the carrier nor a message of a bound buoy. At the count maxSilentWindows the carrier is lost. A buoy counts the same of its shore station, and on the loss begins the resumption of [4.2.6](030-4.2.6-link-resumption.md#4.2.6).

A single window of noise is no loss: the count starts again whenever the carrier is heard.

### 4.2.8 Recovery reporting

After a resumption, or after its failure, the shore station sends a RecoveryReport to the harbour board: the buoy, how long the link was lost, and the last condition heard, with the modifier codes of [TABLE C-2](../annex-c/README.md) where one applies. A CarrierLossReport that was sent meanwhile is closed by it.
