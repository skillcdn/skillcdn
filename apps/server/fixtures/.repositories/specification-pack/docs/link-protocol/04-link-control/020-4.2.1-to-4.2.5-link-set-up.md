## 4.2 Link procedures

Every procedure begins with one message and ends with a state. A station that receives a message it does not expect in its state discards it and stays as it is.

### 4.2.1 Link set-up

A buoy in LISTEN that hears its address in a LinkSetupRequest answers in the next window. The request names the channel and the first guard time. A buoy that hears two requests in one window answers neither.

### 4.2.2 Link confirmation

The shore station confirms with a LinkSetupConfirm. On its receipt the buoy is bound, starts its carrier and reports its condition with the codes of [TABLE C-1](../annex-c/010-table-c-1-station-condition-codes.md#table-c-1).

### 4.2.3 Keep-alive

A bound buoy sends a KeepAlive in every tenth window. The shore station answers none of them: three in a row unheard are the loss of the carrier, which [4.2.7](040-4.2.7-to-4.2.8-carrier-loss.md#4.2.7) treats.

### 4.2.4 Orderly release

Either station ends the link with a LinkRelease that names the reason. The buoy stops its carrier, returns to LISTEN and keeps what it measured until it is bound again.

### 4.2.5 Station hand-over

A shore station that goes off watch hands its buoys over. It sends each a LinkRelease with the reason hand-over and the berth of the station that takes it, and that station sets the link up as in 4.2.1 without waiting for the buoy to ask.
