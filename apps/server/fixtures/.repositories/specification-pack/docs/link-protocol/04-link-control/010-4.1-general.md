# 4 Link control

## 4.1 General

Link control is everything a station does to have a link and to know that it has one. The procedures of 4.2 assume the states and the addresses of this clause.

### 4.1.1 Station states

A station is in one of four states, written DARK/LISTEN/BOUND/HELD wherever a clause names them together.

| State | Meaning | Left by |
|---|---|---|
| DARK | The station neither sends nor listens. | Power-on, or a command of the keeper. |
| LISTEN | The station listens in every window and sends nothing. | A set-up, as in [4.2.1](020-4.2.1-to-4.2.5-link-set-up.md#4.2.1). |
| BOUND | The link is confirmed and the carrier is sent. | A release, a hand-over, or the loss of the carrier. |
| HELD | The link was lost and its resumption is under way. | Resumption or its failure, as in [4.2.6](030-4.2.6-link-resumption.md#4.2.6). |

### 4.1.2 Addressing

A buoy is addressed by the four digits painted on its hull, and a shore station by the two letters of its berth. An address of all zeros reaches every buoy in the window and is never answered.
