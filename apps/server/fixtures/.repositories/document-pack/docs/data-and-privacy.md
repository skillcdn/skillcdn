# Data and privacy

The data of a workspace belongs to the company that runs it. This page says how to take it out, how to have it deleted, and what Tidewell keeps and for how long.

## Export your data

An owner exports everything from Settings: customers, jobs, invoices, payments, notes and the photos technicians took, as a compressed archive of comma-separated files and the photos as image files. The export is prepared in the background and a download link is sent to the owner by email; the link is valid for forty-eight hours. An export can be requested once a day.

## Delete a workspace

An owner deletes a workspace from Settings after typing its name. The workspace is closed at once and deleted thirty days later; within those thirty days the owner can reopen it by signing in and confirming. After the thirty days, every record is gone from the live systems, and from backups as they age out, within ninety days.

## Retention

Cancelled jobs are kept for thirty days and then removed. Everything else is kept for as long as the workspace exists, since invoices and the audit log refer to it. The audit log, which records who changed what and when, is kept for seven years and cannot be edited or shortened.

## Backups

Backups are taken every hour and kept for thirty days. They exist to restore the service after a failure, not to recover a record one person deleted by mistake; for that, a deleted customer or job can be restored from its page within thirty days.

## Where data is stored

A workspace chooses its region when it is created, and its data is stored and processed in that region. Changing the region later means exporting the data and importing it into a new workspace. Messages to customers are sent through providers in the customer's country.
