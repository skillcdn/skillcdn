# Getting started

Tidewell runs a small field-service company from one place: the jobs of the day on a board, the people who do them, and the invoices that follow. This page takes a new owner from an empty workspace to the first completed job.

## Create a workspace

Sign up with a work email address and choose a workspace name; the name appears on every quote and invoice, so use the trading name customers know. The person who creates the workspace is its first owner. A workspace starts on a fourteen-day trial with every feature on, and nothing is charged until the owner picks a plan at the end of the trial.

Set the time zone and the working hours of the company under Settings before scheduling anything: the board, reminders and quiet hours all follow them.

## Invite the team

Under People, enter an email address and pick a role for each person: owner, dispatcher, technician or bookkeeper. The invitation link is valid for seven days; an expired link is renewed from the same page. A technician who only uses the mobile app needs no desk sign-in and is counted as a mobile seat.

People who leave the company are deactivated, not deleted, so that the jobs they completed keep their name.

## The first job

Create a customer from the Customers page or directly from the board, then drag a slot onto a technician's column to make a job. A job needs a customer, a service address, a start time and an expected duration; everything else, such as notes, parts and photos, can be added later from the mobile app.

Mark the job done from the mobile app when the work is finished. A done job can be turned into an invoice with one click; the parts and hours recorded on the job become the invoice lines.

## Install the mobile app

Technicians install the Tidewell app from the store of their phone and sign in with the invitation link. The app shows the jobs of the day, directions to the service address, the customer's phone number and the checklist of the job. It keeps working without a network connection: jobs opened before going offline, and the photos and notes added to them, are synchronized when the connection returns.
