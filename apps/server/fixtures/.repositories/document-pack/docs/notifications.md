# Notifications

Tidewell sends two kinds of messages: reminders to customers about their appointments and invoices, and alerts to technicians about their jobs. Both are written from templates the owner edits.

## Customer reminders

A customer receives a confirmation when a job is scheduled, a reminder the day before, and a message when the technician is on the way, by text message or email as the customer prefers. Invoice reminders go out on the due date and seven days after it, and with every late fee. Each reminder can be turned off per workspace, and a customer can opt out of text messages by replying STOP, which Tidewell honors at once.

## Technician alerts

A technician is alerted when a job is assigned to them, moved, or cancelled, and fifteen minutes before a job starts. Alerts arrive as notifications of the mobile app; a technician without the app gets them by text message. Alerts about a day's jobs are bundled into one message when the dispatcher changes several jobs within a few minutes.

## Quiet hours

No message is sent to a customer outside the quiet hours of the workspace, nine in the evening to eight in the morning by default in the workspace's time zone. A reminder that falls into quiet hours is sent when they end. Alerts to technicians ignore quiet hours, since a technician on call wants them.

## Templates

Every message has a template with placeholders for the customer's name, the technician's name, the time, the address and the invoice total. Templates are edited under Settings, per language of the customer when the workspace has customers in more than one language, and a preview with sample values is shown before saving. A template cannot be emptied: a message with nothing to say is not sent.
