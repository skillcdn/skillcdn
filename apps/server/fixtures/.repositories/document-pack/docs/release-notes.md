# Release notes

What changed, release by release, newest first. Dates are the day the release reached every workspace.

## 2026.3

- The board shows travel time between jobs as a hatched block, and warns when a job does not fit between its neighbours.
- Quotes can ask for a deposit, fixed or a percentage, paid when the customer accepts.
- Webhooks are retried for twenty-four hours and list their failures.

## 2026.2

- Technicians get a read-only calendar feed of their jobs.
- The accounting export marks what it covered and sends corrections for invoices edited after an export.
- Two-step sign-in became required for owners and bookkeepers.

## 2026.1

- Recurring jobs: weekly, every two weeks, monthly or custom, created ninety days ahead.
- Credit notes, with their own numbers, for correcting an invoice without touching its payment.
- Quiet hours for customer messages, per workspace.

## 2025.4

- The mobile app works offline and synchronizes notes, photos and status changes when the connection returns.
- Late fees as a fixed amount or a monthly percentage, waivable per invoice.
- API keys with scopes, and a flag on keys unused for ninety days.
