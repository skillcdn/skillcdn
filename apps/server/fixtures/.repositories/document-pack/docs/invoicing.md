# Invoicing

Money in Tidewell flows from a quote to a job to an invoice to a payment. A bookkeeper or an owner handles everything on this page; dispatchers see invoices but cannot change them.

## Quotes

A quote is an estimate the customer accepts before the work is scheduled. It lists the expected parts and hours with prices from the catalog, and it is sent by email or text message as a link the customer opens and accepts with one tap. An accepted quote turns into a job with the quoted lines already on it; a quote that is not accepted within its validity, thirty days by default, expires and can be sent again.

## Invoices from a job

A done job becomes an invoice with the parts and hours recorded on it; the bookkeeper may edit the lines before sending. An invoice number is assigned when the invoice is sent, not when it is drafted, so that drafts never consume numbers. Numbers follow the format set under Settings, by default a prefix and a counter that resets each year.

## Payment terms

The default payment term is fourteen days from the invoice date; an owner may set seven, fourteen, thirty or sixty days per workspace and change it per customer. The due date is printed on the invoice and drives the reminders and the late fee. An invoice paid by card from the link is marked paid at once; a bank transfer or cash is recorded by the bookkeeper.

## Deposits

A quote may ask for a deposit, as a fixed amount or a percentage of the total, which the customer pays when accepting the quote. The deposit appears on the final invoice as a credit, and a job cancelled after a deposit was paid refunds the deposit from the quote's page unless the owner keeps it as a cancellation charge, which the quote must have said.

## Late fees

A late fee is a fixed amount or a monthly percentage, set per workspace, applied once per month on invoices past their due date. The fee is added as a line to the open invoice, and the customer receives a reminder with the new total. Fees stop when the invoice is paid, and a bookkeeper can waive a fee from the invoice.

## Refunds and credit notes

A card payment is refunded from the invoice, in full or in part, back to the card it was paid with; the refund reaches the customer within five to ten business days, depending on their bank. A payment recorded by hand is refunded by hand, and the bookkeeper records the refund on the invoice so that the balance is right. A credit note cancels all or part of an invoice that was wrong without touching the payment; it carries its own number and refers to the invoice it corrects.

## Sales tax

Tax rates are set per workspace, with a default rate and optional rates per region, and each catalog item is taxable or not. The rate applied to an invoice is the rate of the service address at the time of the invoice, and it is printed as a separate line. A customer marked tax-exempt pays no tax on any invoice, and the exemption number is printed instead.
