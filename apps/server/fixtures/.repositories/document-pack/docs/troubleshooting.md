# Troubleshooting

The problems that reach support most often, and what fixes them.

## Cannot sign in

A person who cannot sign in is usually deactivated, or has two-step sign-in and a lost phone. An owner sees the state on the People page: an inactive person is reactivated there, and two-step sign-in is reset there. A forgotten password is reset from the sign-in page with a link that is valid for one hour. An invitation that says it has expired is renewed by the person who sent it.

## A job is missing from the board

Check the filter first: the board remembers the last filter per person, and a filter on a service area hides jobs elsewhere. Then check the date: a recurring job is created ninety days ahead and no further. A job cancelled by mistake is in the customer's history and can be restored within thirty days.

## Notifications arrive late or not at all

A customer reminder due in quiet hours is held until they end. A customer who replied STOP receives no text messages until they text START. A technician whose phone has notifications turned off for the app gets nothing; the app's settings page shows whether notifications are allowed. Messages the provider could not deliver are listed under Settings, with the reason the provider gave.

## Invoice numbers skip

Numbers are assigned when an invoice is sent. A number appears to be missing when an invoice was sent and then voided: a voided invoice keeps its number and is listed under Voided, so that the sequence has no gaps for the accountant. Drafts have no numbers.

## The mobile app is offline

The app shows an offline banner when it cannot reach Tidewell. Jobs opened before going offline stay available, and notes, photos and status changes are kept on the phone and synchronized when the connection returns, in the order they were made. A job assigned while the technician was offline appears when the app is back online; the dispatcher sees in the job's history when the technician's phone last synchronized.
