# The library of the MMCC and the MSD

The English-language documents of the Office of Research and Defense Materiel come from the Marrowfield Materials Characterization Centre (MMCC), the Office's secretariat, and from the Materiel Standards Directorate (MSD) of the Vendrel Federal Defense Force. Each document states its own facts; none is a translation of another.

## Research documents of the MMCC

| Document | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [Data classification and handling at the Centre](research/data-classification.md) | The four data tiers, approved storage, labelling, the air gap and the write-once media, the transfer log, the release board, retention and destruction |
| [Procurement of goods and equipment for the Centre](research/procurement.md) | The requisition and its approval thresholds, Office Commodity Codes, controlled items, vendors, receiving inspection, asset tags, consumables and returns |
| [Publication clearance and open-access deposit](research/publication-clearance.md) | The pre-publication review and its deadlines, abstracts and posters, the patent hold, repository deposit, authorship, data availability statements and theses |
| [The electron microscopy facility](research/electron-microscopy-facility.md) | The SEM, TEM and FIB instruments, booking and user levels, sample preparation, detectors, cryo transfer, beam time, data export and vacuum incidents |
