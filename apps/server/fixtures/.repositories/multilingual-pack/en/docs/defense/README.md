# Defense documents of the Materiel Standards Directorate

The regulations and publications of the MSD on maintenance, supply, test and evaluation and radio voice procedure in the Vendrel Federal Defense Force.

| Document | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [Levels of maintenance and the maintenance request](maintenance-levels.md) | The three levels of maintenance, PMCS intervals, deadline criteria, the maintenance request and its priorities, evacuation, technical manual numbering, controlled exchange and records |
| [Stock numbers and requisitions](stock-numbers-and-requisitions.md) | The structure of a stock number, part numbers and supplier codes, the requisition and its priorities, substitutes, shelf life and condition codes, turn-in and inventory |
| [Test and evaluation of materiel](test-and-evaluation.md) | Developmental and operational testing, the test readiness review, environmental test methods, reliability and availability, logistics support, deficiency reports and audits |
| [Radio voice procedure](radio-voice-procedure.md) | The phonetic alphabet, prowords, call signs, radio checks, the date-time group, net discipline, authentication, the manpack radio's controls and the radio log |
